  1. September 29, 2026v0.1.0

    The network opensLink to The network opens

    Baton is open. The first version has everything needed to pass people between tools, and nothing more.

    The embed

    • One script tag, https://toolbaton.com/embed.js, with your site key in data-key.
    • window.baton.pass({ ctx, target }) shows one card at your success moment. It resolves to false when there is no match.
    • The card is a <baton-card> web component drawn in a Shadow DOM, themable with data-theme and three custom properties: --baton-accent, --baton-radius and --baton-font.

    The network

    • Journey-based matching: what your tool outputs, against what the next tool takes as input.
    • Credits that move one for one. A click sent earns 1, a visitor received costs 1, and every tool starts with 10.
    • Fairness rules from day one: competitors are never paired unless both opt in, duplicate clicks within 24 hours aren't credited, and a visitor can produce at most 3 credited clicks per host per day.
    • Signed /r/<token> redirects for every click, and anonymous visitor hashes that rotate daily. No cookies.
    • Handshakes: mutual, direct pairings on paid plans, ranked first.

    Plans

    • Relay, free: 1 tool.
    • Anchor, $12 a month: 5 tools, handshakes, 90-day stats and the option to hide the Baton mark.
    • Studio, $39 a month: unlimited tools and handshakes, a year of stats, API access and CSV export.
    • The first 100 tools keep double starter credits.

    API

    • GET /api/baton/v1/card?key=&ctx= returns a card, or 204 when nothing matches.
    • Key-authenticated /api/v1/tools and /api/v1/stats for your own tools and numbers.

    Around it

    • Documentation, including the full list of artifacts.
    • Trails: step-by-step routes between free tools, for people rather than makers.
    • A makers page that explains the exchange in two minutes.
