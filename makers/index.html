For makers · you came from a card

Your tool has a success moment. Use it.

The second your tool finishes its job, your user is holding a result and wondering what comes next. Baton fills that moment with one card for the most useful next tool, and other tools do the same for you.

Put your tool on the network Read the two-minute install

01 / 04 · Setup

What happens in two minutes.

  1. 01

    Say what your tool does

    Name, URL, category, and two lists from a shared vocabulary: what people bring to your tool, and what they leave with. That is all Baton needs to match you.

    You get 10 starter credits

  2. 02

    Paste one line

    Add the embed script to your site, then call baton.pass() at the moment your tool finishes its job: file compressed, audio transcribed, logo exported.

    baton.pass({ ctx: "pdf" })

  3. 03

    Watch the first pass

    People finishing on your site see one card for the next tool. People finishing elsewhere may see yours. Impressions, clicks and credits show up in your dashboard.

    One card, never a feed

02 / 04 · The exchange

Send a visitor, earn a credit. Receive one, spend one.

A click you send

+1

Someone finishes on your site, clicks the card, and lands on another tool. You earn one credit.

A visitor you receive

−1

Someone finishes on another site, clicks your card, and lands on your tool. One credit is spent.

Credits move one for one, so there is nothing to bid on and no slot to buy. Every new tool starts with 10, which is enough to be shown before you have sent anyone anywhere.

03 / 04 · Fairness

Rules that keep the track clean.

Recommendation networks fail when they become ad slots or get farmed. These are the rules that stop both, and the docs spell them out.

  1. 01

    No competitors

    Two tools in the same category are never paired unless both opt in.

  2. 02

    No repeat clicks

    A second click from the same visitor to the same tool within 24 hours isn't credited.

  3. 03

    A daily cap

    At most 3 credited clicks per visitor, per host, per day.

  4. 04

    No cookies, no profiles

    Visitors are identified only by an anonymous hash that rotates every day.

  5. 05

    Earn your place

    A tool with 0 credits isn't shown until it sends traffic of its own.

  6. 06

    Handshakes are mutual

    Paid plans can set up direct pairings, but both tools have to agree. Nobody can buy a spot on your site.
