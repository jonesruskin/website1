Getting started

Add your tool, paste one line, call baton.pass() when it succeeds. About two minutes.

You need a tool with a clear moment when it has finished its job, and a page where that moment happens. That is all.

1. Add your tool

Create an account and add your tool from the dashboard. You will be asked for:

  • Name and URL: what people will see on other tools' cards.
  • Category: used to keep competitors apart. Two tools in the same category are never paired unless both opt in.
  • Inputs: what people bring to your tool (a PDF, a photo, an idea).
  • Outputs: what people leave with (a compressed PDF, a transcript, a logo).
  • Card copy: a short title, a line of body text and a button label for the card other tools show about you.

Inputs and outputs come from a fixed list. Picking them well is the single biggest factor in whether your card is shown to the right people: read Picking inputs and outputs if you are unsure.

Your tool starts with 10 credits, so it can be shown before it has sent anyone anywhere.

4. Check it works

Open the page, complete the job your tool does, and watch for the card. A few things to know:

  • You may see no card at first. Matching only shows tools that fit your outputs, have credits and aren't your competitors. On a young network there are fewer to choose from, and that is normal. baton.pass() resolves to false when there is no match.
  • Your own tools are never shown to you. Baton skips any tool owned by the same account as the host.
  • Your dashboard shows the install. The first time the embed renders on your site, your tool is marked as installed.
