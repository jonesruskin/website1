Pricing

Free to pass. Paid to scale.

Joining the network costs nothing. Plans add more tools, direct pairings and longer stats. They never sell credits, and they never buy rank with strangers: a pairing only ranks first when both makers agree. Paid plans start with a 14-day free trial, and you can cancel any time.

How credits work on each plan

One rule, every plan.

A click you send earns 1 credit. A visitor you receive costs 1. That is the whole exchange, and it is the same on Relay, Anchor and Studio. The first 100 tools on the network keep double starter credits.

  1. 01 · Relay

    One tool, starting with 10 credits (20 for the founding 100). Earn one for every click you send, spend one for every visitor you receive. At zero, your tool waits to be shown until it has sent someone.

  2. 02 · Anchor

    The same exchange across up to five tools, each with its own balance. Handshakes let you pair directly with tools that agree, and they rank first.

  3. 03 · Studio

    The same exchange across a whole portfolio, with unlimited tools and handshakes, a year of stats, API access and CSV export of your ledger.

Questions

Before you pay.

The short answers on plans, credits and handshakes. The rest are in the FAQ.

Is it free?

Yes to start, and for good. The Relay plan is free and covers one tool. Every tool starts with 10 credits (20 for the first 100 tools), and credits are earned by sending visitors, never bought.

Paid plans never sell credits or rank among strangers. The one ranking feature, handshakes, only puts two tools first for each other when both makers agree. Beyond that: Anchor ($12 a month) has 5 tools, handshakes, 90-day stats and the option to hide the Baton mark. Studio ($39 a month) has unlimited tools and handshakes, a year of stats, API access with your keys and CSV export of your ledger. Paid plans start with a 14-day free trial.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Your tool stops being shown on other tools' cards until it sends traffic of its own. Nothing else changes: your embed keeps working, your visitors still see cards for other tools, and every click they make earns you a credit.

This keeps the exchange balanced. Everyone receives at about the rate they give.

What's a handshake?

A handshake is a direct, mutual pairing between two tools that have both agreed to it. Handshakes rank first when Baton chooses a card, so they suit partners who really do want to send people to each other.

Handshakes are on the Anchor plan (up to 25) and the Studio plan (unlimited). Because both sides have to agree, you can't buy a slot on a site whose owner hasn't said yes.

Can I remove the Baton mark?

On Anchor and Studio, yes. On Relay, the card carries a small mark. The mark tells visitors where the card came from, and it is how other makers discover the network.
