03 / 10How it works

Three lanes.
One baton.

A relay, run on the attention you already earned. You pass it on, someone passes it to you.

1

Install one tag

Paste a single script tag before the closing body tag. It loads async and never blocks your page. That is the whole integration.

index.html
<script async src="https://toolbaton.com/embed.js"
         data-key="bk_your_key"></script>

2

Call pass() at your success moment

The file is ready. The transcript is on screen. That is the moment. One call and Baton shows one card for the one tool that fits the journey.

done.js
// the file is ready, the transcript is on screen…
baton.pass({ ctx: "pdf" });

3

Earn when you send, spend when you receive

Every click you send earns 1 credit. Every visitor you receive costs 1. You get exactly as much as you give, and you start with 10 credits.

+1A visitor you sent clicked through

−1A visitor you received

=Fair, by construction.

06 / 10Why now

Recommendations already drive about half of new newsletter subscriptions

~50%About 50 percent

of new Substack subscriptions come through its Recommendations and app network.[1]

Newsletters solved discovery with recommendations. Tools never did.

A writer you trust says “read this too” and it works, because it lands when the reader is already convinced. Tools have that exact moment: the second a job finishes. Today it ends in a closed tab.

Baton is the recommendation layer tools never got. Matched by journey, paid in credits, and honest about what it is. It is new, so you will not find customer logos or invented numbers here.

10 / 10Questions

Questions, answered.

Every question
01Is it free?

Yes to start, and for good. The Relay plan is free and covers one tool. Every tool starts with 10 credits (20 for the first 100 tools), and credits are earned by sending visitors, never bought.

Paid plans never sell credits or rank among strangers. The one ranking feature, handshakes, only puts two tools first for each other when both makers agree. Beyond that: Anchor ($12 a month) has 5 tools, handshakes, 90-day stats and the option to hide the Baton mark. Studio ($39 a month) has unlimited tools and handshakes, a year of stats, API access with your keys and CSV export of your ledger. Paid plans start with a 14-day free trial.

02Does it slow my site down?

It shouldn't. The embed is one script tag loaded with async, so it doesn't block rendering, and nothing is drawn until you call baton.pass() at your success moment.

If the script is blocked or slow, calling window.baton?.pass() does nothing and your page carries on.

03What tools are allowed?

Real, working tools with a public URL and a clear job: apps, utilities, templates, courses, anything people finish something with and then take away. Free and paid tools are both welcome.

We don't accept anything deceptive, illegal or harmful, and we can remove a tool that misleads people or games the exchange. We watch the network for tools that break these rules.

04What do my users see?

One small card, at the moment you call baton.pass(). It names the next tool, says in a line why it fits, and has a single button. It carries a small "passed by Baton" mark unless your plan hides it.

It is never a feed, a carousel or a full-screen interruption. You choose whether it sits in the page or appears as a toast, and you can match it to your site with a light or dark theme and three CSS custom properties.

05What if nothing matches?

Then nothing is shown. baton.pass() resolves to false and your page is left exactly as it was. That is the correct behavior: a bad card is worse than no card.

No match usually means no tool fits what your visitor just made, or the ones that do are competitors of yours or have no credits. It is more common while the network is young, and it improves as more tools describe what they take and what they make.

06Can a competitor show up on my site?

No. Two tools in the same category are never paired unless both of them opt in. When you add your tool you choose its category, and Baton keeps rivals away from each other's success moments.
