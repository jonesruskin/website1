Baton is a recommendation network for the **success moment**: the second a tool finishes its job.
A file is compressed, audio is transcribed, a logo is exported. The person is holding a result and
wondering what to do with it. That is when your tool calls `baton.pass()`, and Baton shows **one
card** for the most useful next tool.

## How a pass works

1. Every tool on the network says what people **bring** to it (its inputs) and what they **leave
   with** (its outputs), using one shared vocabulary of [artifacts](/docs/network/inputs-and-outputs).
2. When your tool succeeds, it calls [`baton.pass()`](/docs/embed/pass).
3. Baton looks for a tool whose inputs match what your tool just produced: a compressed PDF goes
   to a tool that takes a PDF. That is journey-based matching.
4. One card appears. If the visitor clicks, they arrive on the other tool through a signed link, and
   [credits](/docs/network/credits) move one for one: you earn 1, the tool they visited spends 1.

If nothing suitable exists, nothing is shown. `baton.pass()` resolves to `false` and your page
carries on.
