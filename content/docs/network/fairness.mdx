A recommendation network is only worth being on if the recommendations are good and the credits
mean something. These are the rules that protect both.

## How a card is chosen

When a host calls [`baton.pass()`](/docs/embed/pass), Baton considers tools that are **eligible**
and ranks them.

A tool is eligible when it is:

- active, and has at least **1 credit**;
- not the host itself, and not another tool owned by the host's owner;
- not blocked by the host, individually or by category;
- not a **same-category competitor** of the host, unless both tools opt in.

Eligible tools are ranked by:

1. **Journey fit**: how well the tool's inputs match what the host just produced.
2. **Handshakes**: accepted, mutual pairings rank first.
3. **Quality**: a smoothed click-through rate. New tools start at the network baseline and move up
   or down as people react to their card, so a strong card earns more exposure and a weak one
   less.
4. **A little rotation**: equally good tools take turns, so the same one doesn't win every time.

Nothing about the ranking can be bought on the free plan. Handshakes are the only paid element,
and they need both tools to agree.

## The rules

### Competitors are never paired

Two tools in the same [category](/docs/network/inputs-and-outputs) are never paired unless both opt
in. Your card won't put a rival's tool in front of your own users by accident.

### Duplicate clicks aren't credited

If the same visitor clicks through to the same tool more than once within 24 hours, only the first
click is credited. They still reach the tool.

### A daily cap per visitor

At most **3 credited clicks per visitor, per host, per day**. Someone clicking every card a tool
shows, over and over, can't be turned into credits.

### Anonymous visitors only

Visitors are identified only by an anonymous hash that rotates every day, so nobody can be
followed from one day to the next. See [Privacy](/docs/network/privacy).

### Earn your place

A tool with **0 credits isn't shown** until it sends traffic. Being on the network means giving as
well as receiving.

### Handshakes are mutual

Paid plans can set up **handshakes**: direct pairings between two tools that both agree to it.
They rank first between those two tools. You can't buy a spot on a site whose owner hasn't agreed.

## How clicks are protected

- **Signed links.** Every card links to a signed `/r/<token>` address. The signature covers the
  impression, the host and the tool shown, and expires after 24 hours. A link can't be forged for
  an impression that never happened, or redirected to a different tool.
- **One click per impression.** The database allows a single click per impression.
- **Redirect, then attribution.** The link takes the visitor to the tool and adds `?ref=baton`
  so the destination can see where they came from.
- **Uncredited is not blocked.** A click that breaks a rule still works for the visitor. It just
  doesn't move credits, and it is recorded as uncredited.

## What we don't do

- No pay-to-rank on free plans.
- No ads in the slot.
- No tracking cookies, and no cross-site profiles.
- No fake testimonials, users or statistics anywhere on this site.

If you think a tool is gaming the network, [tell us](/contact) and we will look at it.
