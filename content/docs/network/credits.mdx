Baton runs on a simple exchange. Nobody bids for a slot and nobody pays to be shown on a free
plan. Attention flows between tools, and credits keep it balanced.

## The rule

| What happens                                            | Your credits |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| A visitor clicks the card **on your site**              | **+1**       |
| A visitor arrives **at your tool** from another's card  | **−1**       |

One click sent earns one credit. One visitor received costs one. There is no exchange rate, no
multiplier and no hidden fee.

## Starting balance

Every new tool starts with **10 credits**, so it can be shown to real people before it has sent
anyone anywhere. The first 100 tools on the network keep **double starter credits**: 20 instead
of 10.

## What earns a credit

Only a **credited click** moves credits. A click still takes the visitor where they wanted to go,
but it doesn't count towards credits when it breaks one of the [fairness rules](/docs/network/fairness):

- A second click from the same visitor to the same tool within 24 hours.
- A fourth or later credited click from the same visitor on the same host in one day.

Credits are settled through signed links that Baton generates for each card, so a click can't be
forged for a card that was never shown.

## When you run out

A tool with **0 credits isn't shown** on other tools' cards until it sends traffic of its own.

That isn't a penalty on your own site. Your embed keeps working, your visitors keep seeing cards
for other tools, and every click they make earns you a credit. As soon as you have one, you are
eligible to be shown again.

## Reading your balance

Your dashboard shows your current balance and the ledger behind it: a line for the starting
credits and one for every click sent or received. The balance is always the sum of the ledger.

## Plans and credits

The exchange works the same on every plan. Plans change what you can do around it:

| Plan   | Price     | Tools     | Handshakes | Stats                  |
| ------ | --------- | --------- | ---------- | ---------------------- |
| Relay  | Free      | 1         | none       | 7 days                 |
| Anchor | $12/month | 5         | up to 25   | 90 days                |
| Studio | $39/month | Unlimited | Unlimited  | 365 days, plus the API |

A **handshake** is a mutual, direct pairing between two tools that both agree to it. Handshakes
rank first when Baton chooses a card. See [pricing](/pricing) for the full comparison.

<Callout type="info" title="Where credits come from">
  Credits are earned by sending visitors, never bought. Paid plans buy more tools, handshakes and
  longer stats, not more credits.
</Callout>
