You need a tool with a clear moment when it has finished its job, and a page where that moment
happens. That is all.

## 1. Add your tool

[Create an account](/sign-up) and add your tool from the dashboard. You will be asked for:

- **Name and URL**: what people will see on other tools' cards.
- **Category**: used to keep competitors apart. Two tools in the same category are never paired
  unless both opt in.
- **Inputs**: what people bring to your tool (a PDF, a photo, an idea).
- **Outputs**: what people leave with (a compressed PDF, a transcript, a logo).
- **Card copy**: a short title, a line of body text and a button label for the card other tools
  show about you.

Inputs and outputs come from a fixed list. Picking them well is the single biggest factor in
whether your card is shown to the right people: read
[Picking inputs and outputs](/docs/network/inputs-and-outputs) if you are unsure.

Your tool starts with **10 credits**, so it can be shown before it has sent anyone anywhere.

## 2. Paste the script

Each tool has a public site key that starts with `bk_`. It identifies your tool and is not a
secret. Put this in the `<head>` of the page where your success moment happens:

```html
<script src="https://baton.run/embed.js" data-key="bk_…" async></script>
```

The script loads asynchronously, so it doesn't block rendering. Nothing appears on the page until
you ask for it in the next step.

## 3. Call `baton.pass()` at the success moment

Call it right after your tool has done its job, not on page load:

```js
// The file is compressed and the download button is on screen.
window.baton?.pass({ ctx: "pdf" });
```

`ctx` says which of your outputs this moment produced. If your tool can produce several kinds of
result, it narrows the match to the one the person is actually holding.

The optional chaining (`?.`) means nothing breaks if the script is slow to load or blocked by an
extension.

## 4. Check it works

Open the page, complete the job your tool does, and watch for the card. A few things to know:

- **You may see no card at first.** Matching only shows tools that fit your outputs, have credits
  and aren't your competitors. On a young network there are fewer to choose from, and that is
  normal. `baton.pass()` resolves to `false` when there is no match.
- **Your own tools are never shown to you.** Baton skips any tool owned by the same account as the
  host.
- **Your dashboard shows the install.** The first time the embed renders on your site, your tool
  is marked as installed.
