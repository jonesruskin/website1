---
title: Organizing the sidebar
description: Groups come from folders; order comes from frontmatter.
order: 2
---

Top-level files appear first. Each folder becomes a sidebar group named after the folder
(`guides` becomes "Guides"). Inside a group, pages are sorted by `order`, then by title.

To rename a group, add an `index.mdx` to the folder: its `title` becomes the group title and
the page itself is listed first.
