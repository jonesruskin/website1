## What we collect

- **Information you give us**, such as your name and email address when you contact us,
  subscribe to updates or create an account.
- **Usage information**, such as pages visited, approximate location derived from your IP
  address, browser and device type, collected only where analytics are enabled and, where the
  law requires it, only with your consent.
- **Technical data** needed to run the service securely, such as server logs, which are kept
  for a limited time.

## Why we use it

We use personal data to provide and improve the service, to respond to you, to send messages
you asked for, to keep the service secure, and to meet legal obligations. Where consent is our
legal basis, you can withdraw it at any time.

## Who we share it with

We use service providers (for example hosting, email delivery and payment processing) that
process data on our behalf under contracts that require them to protect it. We do not sell
personal data.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize it.
