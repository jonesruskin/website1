Cookies are small files stored by your browser. {legal.siteName} uses them as described below.

## The {legal.siteName} embed

The recommendation card that tool makers add to their own sites sets **no cookies**. See the
[privacy policy](/legal/privacy) for what it records instead.

## Strictly necessary

Needed for the site to work: remembering your color theme, keeping you signed in and protecting
forms from abuse. These don't require consent.

## No analytics or marketing cookies

We don't use advertising, marketing or cross-site tracking cookies. If we measure how the site is
used, we do it with a cookieless analytics tool that doesn't identify you, which is why there is
no cookie banner.

## Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the strictly necessary ones
will sign you out and may stop forms from working.
